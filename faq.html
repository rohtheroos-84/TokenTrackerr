Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I create an account on TokenTrackerr?

Creating an account is simple! Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner, fill in your details, and verify your email address. You'll then have access to all our tracking and portfolio management features.

Is TokenTrackerr free to use?

Yes, TokenTrackerr offers a free basic plan with essential features. We also offer premium plans with advanced analytics, real-time alerts, and additional portfolio management tools.

Portfolio Management

How do I track my cryptocurrency portfolio?

After logging in, go to the Portfolio section and click "Add Asset." Enter your holdings manually or connect your exchange accounts for automatic tracking. Your portfolio will update in real-time with market changes.

Can I connect multiple exchange accounts?

Yes, TokenTrackerr supports integration with major Indian and international cryptocurrency exchanges. You can connect multiple accounts and track all your holdings in one place.

Market Data & Analytics

How often is the market data updated?

Our market data is updated in real-time for most cryptocurrencies. Price updates, market caps, and volume data are refreshed every few seconds to ensure you have the latest information.

What technical indicators are available?

We offer a wide range of technical indicators including RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, and Bollinger Bands. Premium users get access to advanced indicators and custom alert settings.

Security & Privacy

How secure is my data on TokenTrackerr?

We implement bank-level security measures including 2FA, encryption, and regular security audits. Your personal information and portfolio data are always protected.

What happens to my exchange API keys?

Your API keys are encrypted and stored securely. We only use read-only API access, meaning we can never make trades or withdraw funds on your behalf.

Support & Updates

How can I get help if I have issues?

Our support team is available 24/7 through live chat, email, and our help center. Premium users get priority support with dedicated account managers.

How often are new features added?

We regularly update TokenTrackerr with new features and improvements based on user feedback. Major updates are released monthly, with smaller updates and fixes rolled out weekly.
